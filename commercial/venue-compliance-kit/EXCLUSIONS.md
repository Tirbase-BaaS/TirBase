# What this kit does not cover

- **No legal advice or approval.** The templates are drafts; customer counsel/DPO
  must determine lawful basis, notices, DPIA outcome, retention and transfers.
- **No surveillance-lawfulness decision.** Camera placement, signage, video
  retention and VMS processing remain the operator's responsibility.
- **No raw video processing.** The open runtime is metadata-only; video, if any,
  stays in the customer's VMS.
- **No certification or regulator approval.** No GDPR/UK GDPR, sector,
  fire-safety, licensing or CCTV certification is claimed.
- **No customer-specific DPIA.** The four templates must be completed and signed
  for the customer's venue, purposes and jurisdiction.
- **No processor/subprocessor appointment.** Controller/processor terms and
  transfer safeguards are contractual.
- **No retention beyond the customer's chosen schedule.** The open runtime
  enforces configured ceilings; the customer must choose and review them.
- **No source code, keys or secrets.**
- **No warranty or indemnity** beyond a signed agreement.
