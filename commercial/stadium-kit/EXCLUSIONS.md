# Exclusions

This kit does **not** provide, and must not be presented as providing:

1. **Certification.** No CE/UKCA marking, EN 50133/EN 60839 assessment, fire
   certification, SIL/ASIL rating, ONVIF certification, PCI attestation or
   vendor certification.
2. **A safety case for a real venue.** `safety/` is a template and a worked
   argument for a pilot-class installation. The venue's competent person owns
   the real safety case, its assumptions and its acceptance.
3. **Fire or life-safety engineering.** TirBase does not control fire detection,
   suppression, smoke control, gas detection, emergency lighting or egress
   hardware. Nothing here may be used to argue that it does.
4. **Legal advice.** `authority/` and `review/counsel-review-record.md` are
   templates and a review record. Statutory duties, notification thresholds and
   licensing conditions are for the venue's counsel and the authority to decide.
5. **Insurance cover.** `insurance/` is evidence for an underwriter. It is not a
   policy, a warranty or a statement that cover will be offered.
6. **A substitute for a competent installer.** Electrical, network, mechanical and
   life-safety work must be carried out by appropriately qualified people under
   the venue's permits and procedures.
7. **Source code, credentials or customer data.** None is included. The kit is
   usable without source access by design.
8. **Performance or capacity guarantees.** Capacity figures are planning
   assumptions to be verified at commissioning and by live measurement.

Any claim outside these exclusions requires the vendor's written approval and,
where it touches safety or law, the relevant independent review.
