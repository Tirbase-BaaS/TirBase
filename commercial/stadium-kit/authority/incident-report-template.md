# Incident report — template

Use this form to capture an incident for the venue's own record and, where the
venue's counsel and the authority require it, as the basis of a notification.
It is not legal advice and does not determine notifiability or deadlines.

## Description

What happened, in sequence, with times. State what was observed, not what is
assumed.
