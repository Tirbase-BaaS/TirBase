# Recertification

## Cycle

- Badges are valid for **24 months** from issue.
- Recertification requires:
  1. a 20-question refresher exam at 80% or above, covering changes since issue;
  2. evidence of at least one commissioning or event cycle in the period;
  3. a signed declaration of no safety-critical incident or licence breach;
  4. review of any open findings against the holder's work.

## Lapse

| State | Effect |
|---|---|
| Up to 30 days expired | Refresher exam only |
| 31–180 days expired | Refresher exam plus one supervised commission |
| Over 180 days expired | Full course and practical |
| Revoked | 12-month wait, then full course |

An expired badge cannot sign a commissioning checklist. A venue must re-verify
badges before each season.

## What triggers early recertification

- A serious incident or near miss involving the holder's work.
- A material change to the safety case, gate hardware or firmware baseline.
- A change of employer.
- A finding of a false commissioning record.

## Records

The badge registry carries the new expiry and score. The training record holds the
refresher attendance and the declaration. Recertification is auditable by the
venue and the insurer.
