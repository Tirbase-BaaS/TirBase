# End-of-life policy

## Notice

| Event | Notice to the venue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Last firmware feature release | 12 months before EOL | Plan replacement |
| Last security fix | 6 months before EOL | Confirm replacement date |
| EOL date | At announcement | No new commissioning on the model |
| EOL plus 12 months | — | Model removed from the supported catalogue |

## At EOL

1. The model is removed from `hardware/certified-controllers.md` or
   `hardware/readers.md` and from `bom-template.csv`.
2. Existing installations are listed with a replacement date and an owner.
3. A device may remain in service only with a signed deviation that names the
   compensating controls (spares held, network isolation, enhanced monitoring) and
   an expiry no later than 12 months.
4. The asset register and the venue's maintenance plan are updated.

## Replacement

Replacement uses a currently attested model at the supported firmware. The affected
lanes are re-commissioned and the safety-case evidence index is updated if the
change affects a claim.

## Limits

EOL policy addresses support and security, not fitness for purpose. A device near
EOL should be assessed for reliability during an event as well as for support.
