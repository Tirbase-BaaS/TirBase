# TirBase Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA)

Version 1.0 — 2026-11-11

This Contributor Licence Agreement ("CLA") is a legal agreement between you
("Contributor") and the TirBase maintainers ("Project"). It covers your
contributions to the TirBase project.

TirBase is dual-licensed: the runtime is distributed under AGPL-3.0-only, and
the Project also offers commercial/OEM licences. The CLA gives the Project the
rights it needs to offer both. You keep ownership of your contribution.

> This document is a template, not legal advice. Have counsel review it before
> accepting contributions under it.

- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including source
  code, documentation, configuration, tests, and other material, that you

- **"Project"** means the TirBase repository and the maintainers who steward it.
- **"Submit"** means any form of communication sent to the Project, including
  pull requests, patches, commits, issues, and electronic messages, but
  excluding communication you conspicuously mark "Not a Contribution".

The Project is not obligated to use, merge, or distribute any Contribution.

## 8. Corporate contributions

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer or another legal entity, the
entity must agree to this CLA through an authorised signatory. The entity must
also identify the employees or contractors authorised to submit Contributions
on its behalf. Contact the maintainers to arrange a corporate CLA.

## 9. How to sign

For an individual contribution, comment on your first pull request with:

```text
I have read the TirBase CLA and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

Include your full legal name and, if different from your commit identity, the
e-mail address you use for contributions. The maintainers will record the
signature in the pull request.

For a corporate contribution, contact the maintainers before opening the pull
request so the entity-level agreement can be recorded.

## 10. Contribution records

You agree that a record of your Contribution and CLA signature, including the
personal information you provide, may be maintained indefinitely and
redistributed as needed to document the Project's licensing.
