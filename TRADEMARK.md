# TirBase Trademark Policy

The TirBase name, the TirBase wordmark, and the TirBase logo (together, the
"TirBase Marks") are trademarks of the TirBase project. This policy explains
what you may and may not do with them.

The open-source licences in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and
[`LICENSE-APACHE`](LICENSE-APACHE) cover **copyright and patent rights only**.
They do not grant permission to use the TirBase Marks as trademarks.

> This policy is a template, not legal advice. Have counsel review it before
> relying on it in a commercial agreement.

## Permitted uses

You may use the TirBase Marks without prior written permission to:

- refer to the TirBase project truthfully in documentation, articles, talks,
  tutorials, and comparisons;
- state that your product is "built on TirBase", "compatible with TirBase", or
  "uses TirBase" if that statement is accurate;
- distribute an **unmodified** copy of TirBase under its open-source licence,
  retaining the project name;
- use the name in a community meetup, user group, or non-commercial event that
  is clearly about TirBase and does not imply official sponsorship;
- link to the project's repository or website.

## Uses that require permission

You must obtain prior written permission from the maintainers to:

- use the TirBase Marks in the name of a company, product, service, or domain
  name;
- use the TirBase logo in a way that suggests affiliation, endorsement, or
  official status;
- distribute a **modified** version of TirBase under the TirBase name;
- use the marks in paid advertising, commercial packaging, or OEM branding;
- register a trademark, domain name, or social-media handle that is confusingly
  similar to the TirBase Marks.

## Forks and modified versions

If you fork or modify TirBase, you may redistribute the code under the
applicable open-source licence, but you must not present the result as the
official TirBase project. A fork should use a different name and should state
clearly that it is "a fork of TirBase". You may retain factual references to
TirBase in your documentation.

## Commercial and OEM use

Commercial licensing of the TirBase runtime does not automatically grant
trademark rights. OEM partners may be granted limited co-branding or
"powered by TirBase" rights in a separate written agreement.

## Reporting misuse

If you see a use of the TirBase Marks that appears to violate this policy,
contact the maintainers through the repository issue tracker.

## No endorsement

Use of the TirBase Marks does not imply that the TirBase project endorses your
product, service, or organisation.
